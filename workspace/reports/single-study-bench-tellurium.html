Claims
  • H1 holds. At rtol 1e-12 / atol 1e-14 every compared state of all 8 models agrees with libroadrunner within 1e-6 of its scale. Worst per model: BIOMD5 4.6e-9, 10 1.6e-10, 12 2.8e-10, 41 9.6e-12, 254 1.3e-10, 400 5.5e-11, 700 9.9e-11, 1000 2.4e-10.moderate · 2/5provisional-claim · floor
  • H2 holds. At the default tolerances (rtol 1e-8, atol 1e-10) the worst disagreement is 2.5e-8 to 1.3e-5 (BIOMD5, an oscillator), and it always exceeds the tight-tolerance value by 2 to 4 orders of magnitude. That is integrator error, not a semantic difference. Default-tolerance agreement is therefore about 1e-5 for oscillators, not 1e-8.moderate · 2/5provisional-claim · floor
  • H3 holds. Raising one BIOMD12 parameter by 0.1% (eff 20 -> 20.02) produces a 1.09e-3 disagreement, about 1000 times the threshold. A modelling difference this small is detected.moderate · 2/5provisional-claim · floor
  • Cost is inconclusive. The wall time per workbench run is 1.6 to 2.8 s for OdeProcess and 2.1 to 3.8 s for Tellurium. These are single runs dominated by subprocess start-up, imports and model loading, so they don't measure integration cost.moderate · 2/5provisional-claim · floor

Worst per model: BIOMD5 4.6e-9 (tau 1e-7), 10 1.5e-10, 12 2.8e-10, 41 9.6e-12, 254 1.4e-10, 400 3.5e-9, 700 9.9e-11, 1000 3.4e-10 (tau 1e-8).   • v2 H2 holds. The worst disagreement falls by 3.38 to 4.36 orders from default (rtol 1e-8) to tight tolerances, against a pre-registered 2.5, so it is integrator error. A planted semantic error collapses this ratio to 3-21x (panel). Most of the default-tolerance gap comes from CVODE: BIOMD12 OdeProcess at default vs Tellurium at tight is 5.8e-8, against 2.2e-6 with both at default.moderate · 2/5provisional-claim · floor
  • v2 H3 holds. A +1% change of each model's median-sensitivity parameter, chosen on the oracle side by analysis/select_controls.py, is detected in all 8 models (2.2e-3 to 0.49, at least 2e5 x tau). This shows a typical parameter error is detected. It does NOT show that every error is: the panel found 9 of 199 parameters where a 0.1% change moves the trajectories by less than 1e-6.moderate · 2/5provisional-claim · floor

Limitations
  • Shared-misreading risk: agreement with one comparator cannot exclude an SBML interpretation that the importer and libroadrunner share. bench-copasi addresses this.
  • Platform: libroadrunner 2.10.0 / tellurium 2.2.13.1 on macOS arm64. The lock pins libroadrunner 2.10.0, which has no Linux aarch64 wheel (PyPI only ever shipped one for 2.7.0), so `uv sync` of this lock FAILS on Linux arm64 and no linux/arm64 image is built (viva-template#65). Results are not claimed for libroadrunner 2.7.0 or any other version.
  • t = 0 is not compared: TelluriumProcess emits an empty first row (viva-tellurium#7). The importer's tests check initial values separately. For BIOMD41, 98.9-100% of each species' transient lies in [0, 0.5], so the comparison mostly covers its slow drift.
  • Compared set: states, plus the assignment outputs that Tellurium emits. Boundary-species outputs (BIOMD5 YT/CT, BIOMD700) are not emitted by TelluriumProcess and are not compared here; a continuous roadrunner run agrees with them (panel: 2.6e-9, 1e-11).
  • Coverage: none of the 8 models has a rate-rule-parameter state, and hasOnlySubstanceUnits appears only in a size-1 compartment (BIOMD12). Those conversions are covered by the importer's hand-built tests, not by this study.
  • Sample: 8 models chosen for SBML-feature coverage, not sampled. Even if they were a random sample, 0 failures in 8 would only bound the corpus failure rate at <= 31% (95%). The corpus-coverage study addresses this.
  • Detection: tau_m bounds trajectory disagreement, not parameter error. Some parameters move the trajectories so little that a 0.1% change in them stays under 1e-6 (panel: 9 of 199).
  • Both simulators restart integration each 0.5 interval. Chunked and continuous roadrunner differ by up to 9.9e-9 (BIOMD5), and H1 v1 also holds against a continuous run (panel).
  • Cost is not measured (one run per entry; start-up-dominated wall time).
